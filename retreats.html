Your guides

Held by a small,
devoted circle

Rosa and Pouyan have spent years weaving together the practices that shape this retreat — yoga, sound, breath, plants and the simple art of being present together.

Rosa

Yoga & the wisdom of the elements

With a foundation in yoga, scent alchemy and a deep connection to nature, Rosa creates grounding and heart-opening experiences inspired by the wisdom of the elements. Through movement, plants and mindful practices, she gently guides others back to balance, presence and inner connection.

Her warm and intuitive approach creates a nurturing space where guests can slow down, reconnect with themselves and embrace the beauty of the present moment.

Pouyan

Sound, breath & movement

Pouyan is a music producer and facilitator who creates immersive experiences through sound, movement, voice and breathwork. Inspired by travel, music and spiritual traditions from around the world, his sessions invite deep connection, emotional release, freedom and self-expression.

Through rhythm, energy and presence, he guides transformative journeys designed to awaken creativity, open the heart and reconnect people with themselves.

The Location

Hidden in the hills of Málaga

A peaceful retreat space tucked into the Andalusian countryside, with panoramic mountain and valley views, ancient olive groves, and the Sierra de las Nieves National Park on the doorstep. The exact address is shared with confirmed guests.

A quiet corner of Andalucía

The land is sun-soaked and still. Olive groves run down the hillside, far-reaching mountains frame the horizon, and a quiet pond reflects the sky. A protected national park sits at the edge of the property — home to birds of prey, foxes, and trails that wander deep into the Spanish countryside.

The Mediterranean coast and the towns of Málaga, Marbella, and Ronda are all within a short drive. We arrange airport pickup from Málaga for confirmed guests.

Good to know

Your questions, answered

Do I need to be experienced in yoga or meditation?

Not at all. Our sessions are gentle, grounded and open to all bodies and levels of experience. You’re welcome exactly as you are.

Can I come on my own?

Yes — most of our guests arrive solo. The intimate group of just 12 means new friendships happen quickly and naturally around the table, the fire and the daily practice.

What about dietary needs?

Our menu is plant-based by default — vegetarian and vegan. We’re happy to accommodate allergies, intolerances and other dietary needs; just let us know when you book.

What’s the cancellation policy?

Your 20% deposit is fully refundable up to 60 days before the retreat. After that, deposits are non-refundable but can be transferred to a future date.

How do I get there?

You can reach us by car or by bus from Málaga, or we’re very happy to help you arrange transport. Just get in touch and we’ll make it easy.

Will there be Wi-Fi?

Yes — there’s Wi-Fi at the main house if you need to stay reachable. We gently invite you to unplug, but we won’t make it difficult.

What should I pack?

Light layers, swimwear, comfortable clothes for movement, a refillable water bottle and an open heart. We’ll send a full packing list with your welcome pack once you’ve booked.

Is there a private tent option?

Yes — we have a limited number of private bell tents available as an upgrade for +€180. Mention it when you reach out and we’ll let you know if there’s still availability.
