The Location

Costa del Soul — Guaro, Málaga

Costa del Soul is a peaceful retreat space hidden in the Andalusian hills outside Guaro, Málaga — surrounded by ancient olive trees, waterfalls, mountains and wild flowers, with a geodesic yoga dome, a sunlit pool, jacuzzi, and a small family of peaceful donkeys grazing nearby.

The land is sun-soaked and quiet. Guests sleep in comfortable glamping tents, share meals beneath the stars, and wake to the sound of birds and the smell of rosemary. It is a short drive from the coast and the towns of Marbella, Málaga and Ronda.

Address
