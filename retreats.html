The Location

Hidden in the hills of Málaga

A peaceful retreat space tucked into the Andalusian countryside, with panoramic mountain and valley views, ancient olive groves, and the Sierra de las Nieves National Park on the doorstep. The exact address is shared with confirmed guests.

A quiet corner of Andalucía

The land is sun-soaked and still. Olive groves run down the hillside, far-reaching mountains frame the horizon, and a quiet pond reflects the sky. A protected national park sits at the edge of the property — home to birds of prey, foxes, and trails that wander deep into the Spanish countryside.

The Mediterranean coast and the towns of Málaga, Marbella, and Ronda are all within a short drive. We arrange airport pickup from Málaga for confirmed guests.

Facilities & amenities

  • Wisdome — geodesic dome Round light-filled sanctuary for yoga, breathwork and sound (seats up to 30)
  • Shadome — hand-built ceremony space An intimate hand-built structure for cacao, sound and closing circles (seats up to 25)
  • Outdoor amphitheatre stage Open-air gathering space for music, performance and group sharing (capacity 55)
  • Swimming pool & jacuzzi Sunlit pool (1.2–1.4m) and 4-person jacuzzi with outdoor shower
  • Finnish sauna A traditional wood-heated sauna for deep heat and recovery
  • Bell tent glamping Six spacious 24m² tents with quality beds, electricity and AC. One smaller tent and a campsite for vans.
  • Chef’s kitchen & Moroccan lounge Fully equipped kitchen, long shared tables, and a sunken Moroccan-inspired lounge for tea and conversation
  • Sierra de las Nieves trails A protected national park at the doorstep — hiking, waterfalls, birds of prey and wild foxes
  • Resident donkeys Gentle companions for slow morning walks and animal-assisted grounding
