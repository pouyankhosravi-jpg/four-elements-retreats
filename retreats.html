The Location

Hidden in the hills of Málaga

A peaceful retreat space tucked into the Andalusian countryside — surrounded by ancient olive trees, waterfalls, mountains, wild flowers, and a small family of peaceful donkeys. The exact address is shared with confirmed guests.

A quiet corner of Andalucía

The land is sun-soaked and still. Olive groves run down the hillside; waterfalls hide a short walk away; the sea is less than an hour by car. Guests sleep in comfortable glamping tents, share long meals beneath the stars, and wake to birdsong and the scent of rosemary.

It is a short drive from the coast and the towns of Málaga, Marbella, and Ronda — close enough to fly in easily, far enough to forget the world for a few days.
