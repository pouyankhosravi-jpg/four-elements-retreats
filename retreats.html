An invitation

Your soul knows when
it’s time to slow down

This is your invitation to step away from the noise and immerse yourself in four days of connection, movement, healing and transformation in the heart of the Andalusian mountains.

Think slow mornings, moving meditations, breathwork, yoga, hiking, ceremonies, singing under the stars, soulful conversations, dancing barefoot in nature and reconnecting with yourself surrounded by beautiful like-minded souls.

Days filled with sunshine, peaceful nature, donkeys wandering nearby, olive trees, fresh mountain air, swimming pool and jacuzzi moments, and evenings gathered together around long candlelit dinners beneath the stars.

You’ll stay in beautiful luxury glamping bell tents with proper beds and cosy comforts, falling asleep to the sounds of nature and waking up surrounded by mountains and stillness.

Nourishing food will be created daily by a professional chef using fresh, vibrant ingredients designed to feed both body and soul.

Guided by the beautiful energy of Rosa and Pouyan, this intimate Four Elements Retreat is a space to breathe deeply, open your heart and return to what truly matters — while reconnecting with the four elements of nature.

Your guides

Held by a small,
devoted circle

Rosa and Pouyan have spent years weaving together the practices that shape this retreat — yoga, sound, breath, plants and the simple art of being present together.

Rosa

Yoga & the wisdom of the elements

With a foundation in yoga, scent alchemy and a deep connection to nature, Rosa creates grounding and heart-opening experiences inspired by the wisdom of the elements. Through movement, plants and mindful practices, she gently guides others back to balance, presence and inner connection.

Her warm and intuitive approach creates a nurturing space where guests can slow down, reconnect with themselves and embrace the beauty of the present moment.

Pouyan

Sound, breath & movement

Pouyan is a music producer and facilitator who creates immersive experiences through sound, movement, voice and breathwork. Inspired by travel, music and spiritual traditions from around the world, his sessions invite deep connection, emotional release, freedom and self-expression.

Through rhythm, energy and presence, he guides transformative journeys designed to awaken creativity, open the heart and reconnect people with themselves.

The Kitchen

Food that holds you
as much as it feeds you

Chris is our passionate professional chef who believes food is an essential part of connection and wellbeing. Using fresh local ingredients and inspiration gathered through his travels, he creates vibrant and nourishing meals designed to support both body and soul throughout the retreat.

From beautiful dinners beneath the stars to a hands-on paella cooking experience, his food brings warmth, joy and meaningful moments shared together around the table.

Vegetarian and vegan menus, made with fresh, vibrant Andalusian ingredients.

The Location

Hidden in the hills of Málaga

A peaceful retreat space tucked into the Andalusian countryside, with panoramic mountain and valley views, ancient olive groves, and the Sierra de las Nieves National Park on the doorstep. The exact address is shared with confirmed guests.

A quiet corner of Andalucía

The land is sun-soaked and still. Olive groves run down the hillside, far-reaching mountains frame the horizon, and a quiet pond reflects the sky. A protected national park sits at the edge of the property — home to birds of prey, foxes, and trails that wander deep into the Spanish countryside.

The Mediterranean coast and the towns of Málaga, Marbella, and Ronda are all within a short drive. We arrange airport pickup from Málaga for confirmed guests.
