About us

A small team devoted
to the elements

Four Elements Retreats is born from a love of nature, music, movement and meaningful human connection — an invitation to slow down and journey through the elements of Earth, Fire, Water and Air.

Where it began

What began as a quiet conversation between friends has grown into a place — a gathering, a practice, a return. Tucked into the Andalusian hills, surrounded by olive trees, waterfalls, mountains, and peaceful donkeys, this retreat is the result of years of shared journeys through music, movement, plant medicine, and inner work.

We created Four Elements Retreats to share what has shaped us — the sound, the silence, the meals around a long wooden table, and the simple act of being present together in nature.

Inspired by the elements

Earth grounds us. Water moves us. Fire awakens us. Air clears us. Each day of the retreat is shaped around one element — through grounded yoga, breathwork, cacao ceremony, voice activation, sound journeys, hiking, swimming, music, and meaningful conversation.

It is not about doing more. It is about returning — to your body, to your breath, to your natural rhythm.

What guides us

Warmth over performance. Presence over polish. Intuition over rigid structure. We believe in slow mornings, honest meals, deep listening, and the quiet power of spending a few days with a small group of kind people in a beautiful place.

Meet the Team

A small team of facilitators, creators and guides who bring nature, movement, music, nourishment and human connection together with care and warmth. All bodies, all ages and all levels of experience are welcome.

Pouyan

Pouyan is a music producer and facilitator who creates immersive experiences through sound, movement, voice and breathwork. Inspired by travel, music and a love of bringing people together, his sessions invite deep connection, emotional release and self-expression.

Through rhythm, energy and guided movement, he creates spaces designed to awaken creativity and reconnect people with themselves.

Rosa

With a foundation in yoga, scent alchemy, and a deep connection to nature, Rosa creates grounding and heart-opening experiences inspired by the wisdom of the elements. Through movement, plants, mindfulness, and intuitive practices, she gently guides others back to balance, presence, and inner connection.

Her warm and nurturing approach creates a safe and welcoming space where guests can slow down, reconnect with themselves, and embrace the beauty of the present moment.

Chris

Chris is a passionate professional chef who believes food is an essential part of connection, wellbeing and shared experience. Using fresh local ingredients and inspiration gathered through his travels, he creates vibrant, plant-rich Mediterranean meals designed to support both body and energy throughout the retreat.

From beautiful dinners beneath the stars to a hands-on paella cooking experience, his food brings warmth, joy and meaningful moments shared together around the table.

Kasia

Kasia is a hospitality professional and event organiser who brings warmth, care, and positive energy to every part of the retreat experience. She will be your main point of contact throughout the journey, ensuring everything flows smoothly and supporting you with anything you may need.

She also captures beautiful moments and memories throughout the retreat, helping create a welcoming atmosphere where everyone feels relaxed, supported, and truly at home.
